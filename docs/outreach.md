# Backlink Outreach Kit — calculator.co.tz

A practical kit for building external links. **You** send/submit; this gives you the targets,
templates and tracking. Goal quality over quantity — 15–25 relevant, active targets.

> ⚠️ Directory URLs and blogs change. Always verify each target is still live and has a real
> submission/contact page before reaching out. Never buy links or mass-spam.

---

## 1. Where to get links

### A. Free tool & calculator directories (form submissions — easiest)
Submit via the site's "Add / Submit your tool" page. These are quick, often relevant links.

| Target | How |
|--------|-----|
| Product Hunt (producthunt.com) | Launch the site as a listed product (needs an account; benefits from a launch post) |
| AlternativeTo (alternativeto.net) | Add your site as a tool |
| Free-online-tool directories | Search `"submit free tool"` · `"add your calculator"` · `"free online tools submit"` |

**Find more with:** `"best free calculators list submit"` · `"unit converter directory"` ·
`"free tools directory add site"`

### B. "Best X" listicle blogs (link / guest request)
These roundup posts link out to tools. Find them, then pitch to be added.

- Search: `"best free calculators"` · `"best unit converter 2026"` · `"best website to convert units"` ·
  `"online tools list recommendation"`
- Angle: a short, friendly email offering your tool as a resource (Template 1), or a guest article.

### C. Tanzania / East Africa (citations + guest posts — higher authority)
Target local finance, tech, business and education sites for Tanzania-relevant links.

- Search: `"Tanzania tech blog"` · `"Tanzania personal finance blog"` · `"Tanzania business directory"` ·
  `"Tanzania education blog"` · `"East Africa finance blog"`
- Angle: pitch a **Tanzania-specific guest article** (e.g. "How Tanzania's PAYE tax is calculated",
  "A practical guide to USD→TZS"), or a resource link.

for your readers, many of whom search for how to do that.

It's free and no signup is needed. Would you be open to adding a link? Happy to do
the same if useful, or answer any questions.

Best,
[Your name]
[calculator.co.tz]
```

Subject: Guest post idea for [site]

Each includes a working calculator from calculator.co.tz so readers can try it live.
I can write [X] words in your style. Are you open to it?

Best,
[Your name]
```

## 5. Rules & best practices
- **Personalize every email** — never send the identical message to many people.
- **One polite follow-up** after 5–7 days, then stop.
- **Don't buy links**, use PBNs, or spam comment sections — risk of a Google penalty.
- Prefer **relevant, active sites**. A few good links beat dozens of spammy ones.
- Track everything; double down on what works.

3. Follow up once on the ones that don't reply; log everything.
